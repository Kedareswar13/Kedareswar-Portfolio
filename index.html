I'm an AI Engineer who loves the whole loop — build a feature, understand exactly how it behaves inside an agent, then prove it works with evaluations.

At RealPage I work on a conversational leasing assistant that helps apartment prospects and residents over chat, SMS, email and voice. Deep hybrid CNN–LSTM encoder architectures for cross-session EEG biometric identification

G. Ajay Kumar, Pattapu Kedareswar, S. M. Saifuddin Sumair, C. J. N. Gandhi, S. K. Ram, B. B. Das

Read the paper ↗ DOI · 10.1016/j.array.2026.101071

Can your brainwaves be your password — even when they're recorded on a different day? Our work tackles cross-session EEG biometric identification, where a person's signals drift between recording sessions. I processed EEG recordings from the BED dataset into subject-identification pipelines, then designed and evaluated multiple deep architectures — CNN+LSTM, BiLSTM and autoencoder–CNN-LSTM hybrids — combining spatial features (CNN encoders) with temporal dynamics (LSTMs) to improve identification accuracy for biometric authentication.
